## 2026-05-28 - Icon-Only Button Accessibility\n**Learning:** Icon-only view toggles in Next.js require proper ARIA attributes for accessibility and title attributes for mouse tooltips to improve both screen reader experience and general usability.\n**Action:** Always add `aria-label`, `title`, and `aria-pressed` to toggle buttons, and wrap related toggles in a container with `role="group"` and its own `aria-label`.
